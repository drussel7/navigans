NAVIGANS

Monthly bulletin of Marconi Sailing Club, since 1957

    Scanned at 300 dpi from the club's bound set and read by optical character recognition.

    Recognition is good but not perfect on sixty-year-old typescript, so an occasional word is misread.

    Instructions

    Choosing a year

    • The compass picks the year. N jumps forward ten years and S back ten. E goes to the next year and W to the one before.
    • Or click the year in the middle of the compass and type the one you want.

    Reading a newsletter

    • Each box is one month. Hover over it to see what is in that issue.
    • Click the box, then Read this issue.
    • Turn the pages with the arrows at the sides, the arrow keys, or by swiping. The × at the top left closes it.

    Searching

    • Type a name, a boat or a trophy in the search box. It looks through every page of every year.
    • Each result shows where the words appear. Click it to read from that page.
    • Clear the box, or use the compass, to go back to a year.

    Documents

    • Documents lists the rule books, AGM papers and other items that are not newsletters. They also appear in their own year.
